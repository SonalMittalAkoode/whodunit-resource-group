On the Farm

A useful crop
in the rotation.

Lentils, peas, and beans fix nitrogen and help break cereal disease cycles. Our farms and grower organization in Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, and Ontario include pulses as part of their cropping systems.

Crop Rotation

Pulses can reduce synthetic nitrogen demand in the following crop.

Soil & Water

Reduced tillage and soil cover are practical parts of Canadian cropping systems.

In Our Operation

Recover value.
Use resources carefully.

We aim to move grain the shortest practical distance from farm to plant to port, and recover more usable product through cleaning.

Food & Ingredients

Pulses offer protein and fibre for traditional foods, plant-based products and Canadian-origin feed.

Product Recovery

By-products
