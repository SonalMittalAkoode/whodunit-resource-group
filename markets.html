Canadian Pulses move widely in bulk trade. We sell to importers, wholesalers, Dal millers, canners, and manufacturers. Middle East: Turkey and Mersin, UAE including Dubai, Iraq, Saudi Arabia. North Africa: Algeria, Sudan, Egypt, Morocco. Also Colombia, the United States, and Europe. Destination port, packing, and documents are named on the offer.

Markets & Logistics

Storage, packing, and shipment.

Storage and load-out, large volumes held in environmentally controlled bulk hopper bins, then filled into bags, totes, or containers for shipment worldwide.

Containers
Containers, 20' and 40', filled from environmentally controlled hopper bins with bagged, tote, or bulk-in-container cargo.
Bulk load-out
Bulk, large volumes held in environmentally controlled hopper bins, then loaded to bags, totes, containers, or vessel as the contract requires.
Incoterms
We quote FCA (Free Carrier), FOB, CFR, CIF, DAP, and other terms as the trade requires. The basis is stated on the offer. Inland freight, fumigation, inspection, and extra documents are itemized when they sit outside the agreed term.
Shipment windows
Availability and load-out timing are confirmed with each offer.
Program shipments
A shipping calendar is agreed at booking; no unconfirmed lead-time guarantee.
