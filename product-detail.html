We handle the Canadian Pulse market classes used in food and feed. Lentils: Red (small and large) and Green (Laird, Richlea, Eston). Peas: Yellow and Green. Beans: Navy, Pinto, Black, and Kidney. Specialty classes only when named on the offer. Availability follows the crop year.

Ask for a current specification sheet and sample. Typical parameters follow Canadian Grain Commission dry-grade moisture unless the contract is tighter: Red Lentils 13% max, Green Lentils 14% max, Peas 16% max, Dry Beans commonly 14–16% in trade. Grade, size, colour, foreign material, splits, damaged seed, and protein are set on the recap. Purity figures such as 99% are a contract spec, not a CGC grade name.

Delivery terms: FCA (Free Carrier), FOB, CFR, CIF, and DAP, as agreed on the offer. Shipping documents and inspection requirements are confirmed against the contract and destination.

Peas

Yellow and Green Canadian Peas.

Canada is the world’s largest Pea producer and exporter. Yellow Peas dominate export volumes; Green Peas serve food and specialty channels. Both are used in food, splitting, milling, protein extraction, and feed.

Yellow Peas
Yellow Peas: the volume class. Colour, size, and bleach or stain are the commercial controls. Whole, split, feed, or milling. Moisture as above unless the recap is tighter.
Green Peas
Smaller market, tighter colour specification. Used in food packing, splitting, and some ingredient applications. Colour and bleaching are inspected closely.
Other Pea types
Maple and specialty Peas when available. Feed Peas for livestock, pet food, and aquaculture programs.
Formats
Formats: whole · split · feed · flour / milling stock (on request)
