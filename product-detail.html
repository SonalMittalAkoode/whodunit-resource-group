Product Specifications

A specification for every offer.

Ask for a current specification sheet and sample. The figures below are typical trade guidance, not a guarantee for an available lot. The contract sets all limits; purity such as 99% is a contract specification, not a grade name.

Usual grades
No. 1 / No. 2 Canada or agreed buyer specification.
Packing
Bulk, bulk-bag totes, client-specific bags, or branded bags.
Lentil moisture
Typical maximum: red 13%; green 14%, or as contracted.
Pea moisture
Typical maximum: 16%, or a tighter agreed limit.
Dry bean moisture
Commonly 14–16% in trade; class and contract determine the limit.
Quality parameters
Size, colour, foreign material, splits, damage, contrasting classes, and protein where specified.

Lentils

Red, green, whole, or split.

Small red is the main volume class; large red is available by crop year. Choose whole red, dehulled whole (football), or split red lentils (masoor dal).

Green market classes
Laird (large), Richlea (medium), and Eston (small). Colour, stain, and damage determine grade.
Specialty lots
French-type / dark speckled, extra-small red, and other named classes when available.
Formats
Whole, dehulled, split; flour on request.
Buyer programs
Dal mills and splitters specify incoming size, moisture, colour, and split quality.

Peas

Yellow and green Canadian peas.

Yellow peas are the principal export class for food, splitting, milling, protein extraction, and feed. Green peas serve food packing and specialty ingredient channels.

Yellow peas
Whole, split, feed, or milling stock. Colour, size, bleach, and stain are specified.
Green peas
Tighter colour requirements; bleaching is inspected closely.
Other types
Maple and specialty peas when available. Feed programs for livestock, pet food, and aquaculture.
Formats
Whole, split, feed, and flour or milling stock on request.

Dry Beans

The class your market needs.

Growing regions and commercial requirements vary by bean class. We source and prepare the class named on your offer.

Navy
White pea beans for canning and baking.
Pinto & black
Pinto for foodservice; black beans for retail and food manufacturing.
Kidney
Dark and light red kidney beans for canning and foodservice.
Other classes
Great Northern, cranberry, and small red on inquiry. Cleaned, sized whole beans and custom pack; milling on request.
