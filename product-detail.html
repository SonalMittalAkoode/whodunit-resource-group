Red lentils

Whole, dehulled whole, and split red lentils for food and ingredient markets.

  • Whole red, football & masoor dal
  • Typical maximum moisture: 13%
  • Colour and split quality to contract
View specifications

Canadian lentils are graded in two CGC classes: Red, and lentils other than red. Small red is the volume class; large red is offered when the crop year produces it.

Formats include whole red, dehulled whole (football), and split red lentils (masoor dal). Contracts usually specify 13% maximum moisture; tighter limits are named on the recap.

We are open to shipping whole red and masoor dal to India, Bangladesh, Turkey including Mersin, UAE, Iraq, Algeria, Sudan, Egypt, Pakistan, and other destinations. Colour, moisture, and split quality are set by contract.

Green lentils

Large, medium, and small green lentils, sourced to your market class.

  • Laird, Richlea & Eston classes
  • Typical maximum moisture: 14%
  • Whole, dehulled & split formats
View specifications

Green lentils sit in the CGC class lentils other than red. Trade names are Laird (large), Richlea (medium), and Eston (small).

Colour, stain, and damage determine the grade. Contracts usually specify 14% maximum moisture. Variety names such as CDC lines change by crop year; the offer states the market class.

Whole, dehulled, split, and flour formats are discussed against your process and the available lot.

Peas

Yellow and green Canadian peas.

Yellow peas dominate Canadian pea export volumes; green peas serve food and specialty channels. Both are used in food, splitting, milling, protein extraction, and feed.

Yellow peas
The volume class. Colour, size, bleach, and stain are the commercial controls. Whole, split, feed, or milling stock. Typical maximum moisture is 16%, unless the recap is tighter.
Green peas
A smaller market with tighter colour specifications. Used in food packing, splitting, and ingredient applications. Colour and bleaching are inspected closely.
Other pea types
Maple and specialty peas when available. Feed peas for livestock, pet-food, and aquaculture programs.
Formats
Whole, split, feed, and flour or milling stock on request.
