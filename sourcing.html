PREPARED TO YOUR SPECIFICATION

Cleaning & Grading

We clean, sort, and grade to contract specification using specialized equipment: aspiration, screening, gravity separation, and optical colour sorting to remove dockage, off-types, damaged seed, and foreign material. The goal is a stable grade that matches the spec, not a pretty sample.

Sizing & Class Separation

We use calibrated screens and length graders so a lot meets a size band, which is important for Lentils, Peas destined for splitting, and canning Beans.

Milling

Pulse flours and grits for bakers, snack producers, pasta makers, and protein/starch programs. Particle size and protein content can be specified when the plant allows.

Packing & Branding

We supply bulk volumes and also pack into bulk-bag totes and smaller client-specific bags. Optional branding is available so the bag suits the buyer and the destination market. Packing is part of the specification, not an afterthought.

Dehulling & Splitting

Removal of the seed coat and splitting of the cotyledon to produce Dal-style products: split Red Lentils, split Yellow Peas, and related formats used across South Asia, the Middle East, and ingredient plants.

Identity-Preserved Programs

Segregated handling for customers who need a defined origin, variety, or process, organic, specific variety, or a multi-shipment program with a locked spec.
