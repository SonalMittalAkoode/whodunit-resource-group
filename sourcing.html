PREPARED TO YOUR SPECIFICATION

Cleaning & Grading

Aspiration, screening, gravity separation, and optical colour sorting remove dockage, off-types, damaged seed, and foreign material. Cleaning and grading aim for a stable grade that matches the contract specification.

Sizing & Class Separation

Calibrated screens and length graders hold the lot within its agreed size band — for lentils, peas destined for splitting, and canning beans.

Milling

Pulse flours and grits for bakers, snack producers, pasta makers, and protein or starch programs. Particle size and protein content can be specified when the plant allows.

Packing & Branding

Bulk volumes, bulk-bag totes, and smaller client-specific bags, with optional branding for the buyer and destination market. Packing is part of the specification and is agreed before processing.

Dehulling & Splitting

Remove the seed coat and split the cotyledon for dal-style products: split red lentils, split yellow peas, and formats used across South Asia, the Middle East, and ingredient plants.

Identity-Preserved Programs

Segregated handling for a defined origin, variety, or process, or a multi-shipment program with a locked specification. Organic lots are offered only from certified supply.
