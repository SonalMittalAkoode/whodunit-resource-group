Canadian-owned and vertically integrated. We farm our own land and work with a large organization of growers in Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, and Ontario under relationships built on Integrity, Honesty, and Trust. We store, process, package, and ship from the same operation. Buyers of every size deal with one counterparty from farm to the bill of lading.

What vertical integration changes for the buyer: specification is set once and held through cleaning, hopper storage, and packaging. Residue and handling protocols can be applied on our own farm and requested across the grower organisation. Shipping is scheduled from our storage bins, not from a third-party facilities. Prices to our clients reflect fewer intermediaries, middle men and a shorter supply chain period.

This allows our customers to receive the highest quality product at the best market price.

We farm ourselves. We stand behind our commodities.

Vertical integration is an operating structure, not a slogan. One company controls farming, intake, environmentally controlled hopper storage, cleaning and grading, packing, and export documentation. That shortens the chain between field and destination, reduces intermediary markup, and keeps specification, residue protocol, and shipment timing under a single responsible party. Offers state the spec before they state the price. Samples are provided when the lot or the first shipment requires them.
