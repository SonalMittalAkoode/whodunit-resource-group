Canadian origin. Standing relationships.

Whodunit Resource Group is a fully vertically integrated, Canadian-owned and operated company based in Alberta. We farm our own land and work with a large organization of growers in Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, and Ontario.

These relationships rest on integrity, honesty, and trust. From that supply, we prepare and ship Canadian pulses directly to buyers of every size.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

Specification set once.

We clean, size, colour-sort, and grade to contract; dehull, split, or mill when required; and pack in bulk, totes, or client-specific bags.

Our Vision

Responsibility held throughout.

Farming, intake, environmentally controlled hopper storage, processing, packing, and export sit with one responsible party. Fewer intermediaries keep the chain shorter.
